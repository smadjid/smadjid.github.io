  <h1>RhetConnect</h1>

  <p>Independent research, extending earlier work on rhetorical structure for
  multimedia documents carried out at CERIST. RhetConnect addresses a
  structural safety problem in multimedia document adaptation: importance-based
  selection can silently orphan units when media-type constraints exclude
  their rhetorical anchor, producing output that is complete in bytes but
  incoherent in meaning.</p>

  <h2>Contributions</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>A formal definition of rhetorical connectivity for multimedia documents
    modeled as Extended Rhetorical Graphs.</li>
    <li>A proof that naive weight-threshold selection is structurally unsafe
    in the general case.</li>
    <li>RhetConnect, a polynomial-time algorithm with proved guarantees for
    rhetorical connectivity, budget compliance, and media-type compliance.</li>
    <li>Dual empirical validation: 270 synthetic experimental pairs and the
    RhetConnect Corpus v1.0, a novel 40-document annotated corpus of real
    multimedia educational documents.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>RhetConnect Corpus v1.0</h2>
  <p>The first publicly available corpus of multimedia educational documents
  annotated with cross-media rhetorical structure: 40 documents, 559
  elementary units, 396 rhetorical relations, spanning 10 academic domains.
  Sourced from CK-12 Foundation open educational resources plus original
  constructed documents. Released under CC BY 4.0 (annotations) and MIT
  (code).</p>
