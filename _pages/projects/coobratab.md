  <h2>Description</h2>

  <p>CoobraTAB is an interactive system that lets blind and visually impaired users explore and analyze complex data tables (financial, epidemiological, scientific) through natural-language dialogue. It ingests tables in a range of formats (HTML, scanned image, PDF, CSV) and reconstructs their full semantic structure — hierarchical headers, merged cells, totalization relationships — so that users can ask questions such as "which are the three companies with the highest revenue?" or "compare France and Germany's results across all quarters."</p>

  <h2>Main features</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Multi-format ingestion</strong> — HTML, images, PDF, CSV, with automatic table-structure recognition.</li>
    <li><strong>Multi-turn dialogue</strong> — handles references ("and for Q3?") and analytical intents (comparison, filtering, trend computation).</li>
    <li><strong>User-profile adaptation</strong> — adjusts to expertise level, type of visual impairment, exploration style, and desired verbosity.</li>
    <li><strong>Screen-reader optimized output</strong> — voice output tuned for JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver, and TalkBack, plus mathematical braille formatting.</li>
    <li><strong>Grounded answers</strong> — responses are anchored in the table's actual data through a grounding mechanism, to avoid hallucination.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Status</h2>
  <p>Prototypes are under development. A multi-phase user evaluation protocol (formative, technical, cross-format comparative) is planned. Open-source repository and online demo links are forthcoming.</p>
